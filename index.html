About

I am a PhD candidate in Hydrosystems Engineering at Arizona State University. My research combines remote sensing, explainable AI, and mechanistic modeling for water cycle and land-surface applications.

I work on image processing and semantic segmentation (including foundational models), interpretability methods (e.g., Spectral Grad-CAM, Inter-class Grad-CAM), data assimilation, and digital twins for Earth observation.
